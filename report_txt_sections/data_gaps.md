### Data Gaps, Limitations & Method Notes  
While assembling this snapshot, a few data gaps and ambiguities arose: 

- **Instagram Metrics:** Instagram now restricts public viewing of profile stats without login. We could not directly confirm Ghost’s follower count or precise engagement figures (❔). Estimates were drawn from contextual clues (e.g., Twstalker for Twitter, typical engagement seen in screenshots) and thus marked ⚠️/❔. A logged-in check or official disclosure would improve precision here. 

- **TikTok Confusion:** The term “Ghost Tequila” on TikTok mostly surfaces content about a different product (Gran Agave’s ghost-shaped bottle) ([www.delish.com](https://www.delish.com/food-news/a37395972/ghost-tequila-bottle/#:~:text=Like%20most%20of%20these%20little,different%20types%20of%20tequila%20here)). We flagged this in the report to avoid conflating the two. This highlights a limitation: social listening for “ghost tequila” is noisy, and manual curation was needed to isolate the brand’s signals. We treated any ambiguous “ghost” mentions as unrelated unless clearly co-mentioned with keywords like “margarita” or “ghost pepper” or the @ghosttequila handle. 

- **Review Aggregation:** We gleaned ratings from platforms like Drizly (via DoorDash) ([www.doordash.com](https://www.doordash.com/products/ghost-spicy-80-proof-blanco-tequila-bottle-750-ml/urpc_f68bc709-a4f1-4d1b-98df-04fcb7e2b315#:~:text=4)) and Distiller ([distiller.com](https://distiller.com/spirits/ghost-blanco-tequila#:~:text=4)), but the **volume of reviews** was sometimes small (e.g., 12 reviews on Distiller). Thus, statements about sentiment are weighted toward qualitative examples over hard quantitative analysis. No single source had hundreds of reviews publicly visible. Where data was sparse, we labeled it accordingly (❔). We did not have access to Total Wine’s or Vivino’s internal review counts if any, due to login/geo restrictions. 

- **Sales Figures:** No exact sales or revenue numbers for Ghost are public. We relied on proxy indicators like distribution deals, case production volumes (6,000 cases of Reposado) ([bevinfogroup.com](https://bevinfogroup.com/2024/03/22/ghost-tequila-unveils-reposado-expression/#:~:text=is%20a%20balance%20of%20spice,and%20sweetness)), and funding news ([www.just-drinks.com](https://www.just-drinks.com/news/ghost-tequila-attracts-funds-names-new-ceo/#:~:text=Ghost%20Tequila%20attracts%20funds%2C%20names,Ghost%20Tequila%20Ghost%20Tequila%20has)) to gauge commercial success. These point to growth but we avoided guessing any dollar sales or market share. 

- **Community Anonymity:** Some sentiment quotes come from anonymous Reddit users. We cited the content with context but, as per policy, left individuals unnamed. The consistency of themes across multiple users adds confidence that these aren’t outlier opinions (✅ when similar remarks appeared in different threads, we leaned on them). 

- **Platform Visibility Issues:** Certain platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn) likely have Ghost-related chatter (e.g., in private mixology groups or industry posts), but those were beyond the scope of open-web scraping. Our focus remained on public or broadly accessible content. It’s possible we missed minor discussions in closed communities. However, major public forums and websites were covered, so any large patterns would likely emerge there. 

**Entity Disambiguation Log:** We encountered multiple “ghost” mentions not related to Ghost Tequila: 
    - *Ghost energy drinks (Ghost Lifestyle)* – excluded.  
    - *“Ghost Kitchen” references* – unrelated to spirits, excluded.  
    - *Ghost the band, Ghost media, etc.* – obviously excluded.  
    - *“Gran Agave Ghost Edition” tequila* – flagged as a different product; not included except to clarify confusion.  
In all cases, we only included “Ghost” mentions when clearly tied to the tequila brand or context (e.g., a cocktail recipe crediting Ghost, a review of Ghost spirits, etc.). Ambiguous cases (like a tweet simply saying “try the ghost tequila cocktail”) were cross-checked; if it clearly pointed to Ghost Tequila brand, we used it, otherwise omitted or noted as ambiguity. These diligence steps ensure the analysis is truly about Ghost Tequila (Ghost Spirits, LLC) and not polluted by homonyms. 

**Confidence Recap:** Key claims have been tagged ✅ where directly observed: e.g., RNDC distribution ([bevinfogroup.com](https://bevinfogroup.com/2024/03/22/ghost-tequila-unveils-reposado-expression/#:~:text=Ghost%20Reposado%20is%20available%20nationally%2C,is%20nationally%20distributed%20by%20RNDC)), average consumer rating ([www.doordash.com](https://www.doordash.com/products/ghost-spicy-80-proof-blanco-tequila-bottle-750-ml/urpc_f68bc709-a4f1-4d1b-98df-04fcb7e2b315#:~:text=4)), press features ([www.maxim.com](https://www.maxim.com/food-drink/national-tequila-day-bottles/#:~:text=but%20lower%20those%20eyebrows%20now%E2%80%94Ghost,aka%20Bhut%20jolokia%29%20before)), record event ([worldredeye.com](https://worldredeye.com/2024/05/ghost-tequila-creates-the-largest-perfectly-spicy-margarita/#:~:text=Eye%20,to%20kick%20off%20the%20summer)). Estimated points (⚠️) are given for follower counts and geographical spread based on partial data. Sparse signals (❔) are noted for things like the exact size of TikTok content, where evidence was weak or anecdotal. No assertions were made without some supporting evidence. In cases of doubt, we erred on the side of transparency about not knowing (for example, not guessing exact follower numbers or sales figures).
