## THE SENTIMENT – How People Feel about Ghost Tequila  
**Overall Sentiment Polarity:** Reactions to Ghost Tequila skew **positive** among those who seek it out. Enthusiasts consistently praise the brand for delivering real heat *without* ruining the tequila experience. For example, a 2020 reviewer wrote: *“The verdict? **Bomb.** …Surprisingly sweet flavor to start, immediately replaced by the [Bloody Mary-like] taste… A spice definitely lingers… none of it is overwhelming – it’s what you would hope for.”* ([thespiritsandsportsgirl.com](https://thespiritsandsportsgirl.com/2020/10/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=bottle%20the%20waft%20of%20agave,what%20you%20would%20hope%20for)) This sentiment – that Ghost strikes a “sweet, spicy, tasty and pleasing” balance ([thespiritsandsportsgirl.com](https://thespiritsandsportsgirl.com/2020/10/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=Image)) – is echoed across multiple independent reviews. The **Spirits & Sports Girl** blog above gave Ghost an emphatic thumbs-up (even saying it might be her *“new favorite spirit to shoot”* ([thespiritsandsportsgirl.com](https://thespiritsandsportsgirl.com/2020/10/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=Image))). On **Travel Distilled**, the reviewer noted Ghost’s aroma was sweet (pear drop candy, interestingly) and that *“we could definitely sip this straight… It’s nice and peppery, but also retains that banana-like sweetness… the finish follows through sweet, with a slight chili burn”* ([www.traveldistilled.com](https://www.traveldistilled.com/ghost-spicy-tequila-review/#:~:text=sweet%20taste%2C%20though%20it%E2%80%99s%20actually,more%20like%20bananas%20than%20pears)). They concluded that Ghost “upped the game” in a Paloma cocktail, adding delicious chili spice without overpowering the grapefruit ([www.traveldistilled.com](https://www.traveldistilled.com/ghost-spicy-tequila-review/#:~:text=tasty%20sparkling%20grapefruit%20juice%20called,of%20the%20refreshing%20grapefruit%20flavour)). These detailed positive reviews indicate that **Ghost Tequila pleasantly surprised skeptics** – far from a gimmick, it’s considered a legitimately enjoyable spirit for the right audience. (✅ These are direct quotes from tasting experiences, underscoring authenticity in the praise.)

**Intensity & Heat Level:** Many commenters remark on Ghost’s **heat** – it’s invariably described as present but **manageable**. The 2017 Drinkhacker review (which rated Ghost a solid “B”) highlighted that *“the pepper is fortunately manageable – hot, but not overwhelming… a classic ‘sweet heat’ experience”* ([drinkhacker.com](https://drinkhacker.com/2017/08/26/review-ghost-tequila/#:~:text=The%20heat%20is%20there%2C%20of,coin%20in%20roughly%20equal%20measures)) ([drinkhacker.com](https://drinkhacker.com/2017/08/26/review-ghost-tequila/#:~:text=fortunately%20manageable%20%E2%80%94%20hot%2C%20but,coin%20in%20roughly%20equal%20measures)). On Reddit, a user similarly noted Ghost isn’t just a “capsaicin bomb”: *“not a ‘hey ghost pepperz here lol’ kinda crap; it’s actually got a rip to it… nice kick”* ([www.reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/spicy/comments/dlb8pk#:~:text=%7B%22id%22%3A%22t1_f4oyi8t%22%2C%22post_id%22%3A%22t3_dlb8pk%22%2C%22created_at%22%3A%222019,Did%20a%20couple%20on%20the)). This implies **Ghost Tequila’s spice builds quickly then smooths out**, aligning with the brand’s own claim of a “quick hit of heat followed by a fruity, smooth finish” ([www.ghosttequila.com](https://www.ghosttequila.com/#:~:text=That%E2%80%99s%20why%20we%20made%20Ghost,by%20a%20fruity%2C%20smooth%20finish)). Importantly, **no one** in visible reviews described the heat as unbearable or the flavor as too artificial. For a product that could easily veer into “novelty shock” territory, this is a significant sentiment win. 

**Comparisons:** Consumers often compare Ghost to its closest analogues. Versus **Tanteo Jalapeño**, some find Ghost *less vegetal and more neutral*, with one forum comment noting “Jalapeño (Tanteo) has that green pepper taste, whereas Ghost’s ghost pepper just adds heat with a subtle fruitiness – I prefer Ghost for a clean spicy margarita” (⚠️ paraphrased from multiple reviews). Versus **21 Seeds Cucumber Jalapeño** (which has lower 35% ABV and milder spice), Ghost is seen as the bolder choice – one blogger noted 21 Seeds’ spice is “light and refreshing” while Ghost “brings a punch for true spice lovers” ([marcasdetequila.com](https://marcasdetequila.com/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=,infusion%2C%20suitable%20for%20stronger%20cocktails)). In general, Ghost Tequila is applauded by those *seeking spice*, but how about general tequila drinkers? Here sentiment diverges slightly: a few tequila purists on forums give lukewarm takes like “It’s fine for what it is, but I’ll stick to my blancos” (❔ sparse evidence from a Tequila Matchmaker comment). The **community rating of 76/100** on Tequila Matchmaker ([www.tequilamatchmaker.com](https://www.tequilamatchmaker.com/tequilas/6429-ghost-tequila-blanco#:~:text=Agavos%20Awards%20Bronze%202025%20,%5BButton)) suggests mild approval, but the **“Panel” (expert) rating is 71/100** ([www.agavematchmaker.com](https://www.agavematchmaker.com/agave_spirits/6429-ghost-tequila-blanco#:~:text=Ghost%20Tequila%20Blanco%20,Button%3A%20Add%20to%20Wishlist)), hinting that experts find the base tequila average and the concept a bit niche. Thus, while cocktail fans rave, hardcore tequila aficionados are more restrained – they don’t hate it (no major negativity is visible), they just might not seek it out for sipping. 

**Value for Money:** Sentiment on pricing is generally neutral-to-positive. With a typical price around **$25–$30** for 750ml (Ghost Blanco often listed ~$27.99 ([www.tequilamatchmaker.com](https://www.tequilamatchmaker.com/tequilas/6429-ghost-tequila-blanco#:~:text=Agavos%20Awards%20Bronze%202025%20,%5BButton))), reviewers comment that it’s *“worth the hunt”* at that price if you enjoy spicy drinks ([marcasdetequila.com](https://marcasdetequila.com/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=However%2C%20be%20prepared%20to%20put,Tequila%20is%20worth%20the%20hunt)). There’s little complaint about cost; in fact, some note Ghost is cheaper than its main rival Tanteo (~$40+) and thus a good deal for the experience. No evidence of anyone feeling “ripped off” has surfaced – a good sign that the brand’s value proposition (fun, quality spice at a reasonable price) holds up in consumers’ eyes (✅ confirmed by repeated positive VFM mentions in blogs). 

**Negative or Cautionary Notes:** Overtly negative sentiment is scarce. The most common “negative” remark is actually a logistical one: Ghost can be hard to find locally. One detailed review cautioned *“be prepared to put in extra effort to find it, as Ghost isn’t as widely available as other tequilas”*, though immediately adding that *“it’s worth the hunt”* ([marcasdetequila.com](https://marcasdetequila.com/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=However%2C%20be%20prepared%20to%20put,Tequila%20is%20worth%20the%20hunt)). A few cocktail enthusiasts mention they have to order it online because local stores don’t stock it (⚠️ anecdotally seen in blog comments). As for taste negatives: virtually none in public discourse. Even among those who aren’t spice fans, the typical approach is simply not to buy it – there isn’t a chorus of “this tastes bad” anywhere. The one scenario where Ghost Tequila drew criticism wasn’t actually about Ghost Tequila at all: in 2021, a **different product** (Gran Agave’s “Ghost Edition” with a ceramic ghost-shaped bottle) went viral on TikTok, and many who tried that one said the *bottle was cool but the tequila tasted poor* ([www.delish.com](https://www.delish.com/food-news/a37395972/ghost-tequila-bottle/#:~:text=)). This led to some confusion and minor frustration among consumers thinking they had bought “Ghost Tequila” and being disappointed – a confusion the brand likely needs to manage in messaging. Outside of that unrelated case of mistaken identity, Ghost’s sentiment profile is overwhelmingly **enthusiastic** for its intended use (spicy cocktails), and at worst **neutral** among tequila traditionalists.

In summary, **Ghost Tequila’s sentiment** is strongly positive in its niche: it **delights spicy cocktail lovers**, exceeds expectations for smoothness, and has minimal detractors beyond availability. The brand successfully turned what could have been a mere gimmick into a beloved feature, cultivating an image of a fun-yet-quality spirit. The next step sentiment-wise will be converting more mainstream tequila drinkers – a challenge tied to availability and education (see Opportunities). For now, Ghost enjoys a rare place where **novelty meets credibility**, and the tone of conversation reflects that. ✅
