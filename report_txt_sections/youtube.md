## INSTAGRAM & SOCIAL – Primary Channels Deep Dive  
**Instagram (@ghosttequila)** – the brand’s social hub – showcases Ghost’s identity as the “Perfectly Spicy” tequila. The profile features cocktail photos, recipe Reels, and branded campaigns (e.g. the #GhostYourValentine challenge) in a bold, playful aesthetic. The account is not verified and follower counts are hidden without login (⚠️ estimated in the low five figures based on engagement patterns), but it’s clearly active: recent posts highlight spicy margarita variations and user-submitted cocktail ideas. Ghost’s Instagram cadence appears moderate (roughly weekly posts, with bursts around holidays or product launches). Content leans heavily into **cocktail culture** – e.g. recipe videos, bartender collaborations (one signature **Spicy Margarita** recipe is credited to bartender @BadBirdy ([www.ghosttequila.com](https://www.ghosttequila.com/#:~:text=Red%20Flag))), and repurposed memes about “regular margaritas being embarrassing” (a tagline from their site ([www.ghosttequila.com](https://www.ghosttequila.com/#:~:text=Regular%20margaritas))). Hashtags like **#GhostTequila** and **#GetGhosted** are used in posts and encouraged for fans. 

Engagement on Instagram is respectable for a niche spirits brand: recent public posts (sampled qualitatively) show on the order of **hundreds of likes and a dozen comments each** (❔ exact averages unverified due to Instagram’s interface). Fans tag the brand in cocktail photos – e.g. home mixologists showing off **Ghost Pepper margaritas** garnished with chili rims. Comment sentiment on IG is largely upbeat (pepper emojis and “🔥🔥” are common), reflecting a small but enthusiastic community (✅ confirmed via brand-tagged posts surfacing in search ([www.reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/alcohol/comments/iw4oa9#:~:text=2020,1.0))). 

**Twitter/X (@ghosttequila)** – Ghost’s presence on X is modest, with just **621 followers** as of the last scrape ([twstalker.com](https://twstalker.com/Tequila_Fan#:~:text=Ghost%20Tequila%20%40ghosttequila%20621%20Followers,Available%20%F0%9F%8D%B9%2021%2B%20GHOST%20Responsibly)). The bio reiterates the “🌶 Perfectly Balanced Spicy Tequila” tagline and hashtags #getghosted #ghosttequila ([twstalker.com](https://twstalker.com/Tequila_Fan#:~:text=Ghost%20Tequila%20%40ghosttequila%20621%20Followers,Available%20%F0%9F%8D%B9%2021%2B%20GHOST%20Responsibly)). Activity on Twitter appears sparse; no high-profile tweets were noted during research, suggesting this channel is not a major focus. Similarly, a **Facebook** page exists (likely under “Ghost Tequila”), but no significant engagement or recent posts were publicly visible (❔ likely low activity – e.g. just basic info or duplicated content from IG). 

**TikTok & YouTube (Earned Media):** Ghost Tequila does not run a prominent official TikTok (no verified brand account found). Instead, the brand is present via *earned* content – cocktail creators and spice-challenge enthusiasts featuring Ghost in their videos. For example, in February 2024 Ghost ran a campaign inviting users to post ex-inspired cocktails with #ExDrinksWithGhostTequila on TikTok ([chilledmagazine.com](https://chilledmagazine.com/must-mix-ghost-your-valentine/#:~:text=Channel%20your%20inner%20love%20alchemist,let%20the%20mixology%20magic%20unfold)). A search on TikTok’s hashtag shows scattered user videos mixing Ghost into spicy margaritas (✅ small-scale UGC). On **YouTube**, Ghost appears in independent reviews and tasting videos: e.g. bloggers like “The Spirits & Sports Girl” and **Travel Distilled** have dedicated Ghost Tequila review videos or posts (often cross-posting on YouTube) where they sample the tequila and make cocktails ([www.traveldistilled.com](https://www.traveldistilled.com/ghost-spicy-tequila-review/#:~:text=If%20there%20was%20little%20spice,version%20of%20a%20Bloody%20Mary)) ([thespiritsandsportsgirl.com](https://thespiritsandsportsgirl.com/2020/10/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=The%20verdict%3F%20Bomb,what%20you%20would%20hope%20for)). These earned posts usually garner a few thousand views – niche but targeted reach among spirits enthusiasts. Ghost’s own YouTube presence is negligible (no official channel with content, ❔ if one exists it’s inactive). 

**Cross-Platform Integration:** Ghost leverages Instagram as the content engine, then amplifies via press and community challenges on other platforms. Campaigns such as **#GhostYourValentine** were featured on Instagram and then picked up by digital magazines (Chilled, etc.), encouraging participation on **TikTok and Instagram** alike ([chilledmagazine.com](https://chilledmagazine.com/must-mix-ghost-your-valentine/#:~:text=Channel%20your%20inner%20love%20alchemist,let%20the%20mixology%20magic%20unfold)). The brand’s Spotify playlists (linked via their Lnk.Bio ([lnk.bio](https://lnk.bio/ghosttequila#:~:text=%40ghosttequila))) and the use of trendy slang in campaigns (“ghosting” in dating context) indicate an attempt to stay culturally relevant to a younger audience across platforms. Overall, Ghost’s owned social footprint is strongest on **Instagram (✅ active)**, minimal on **Twitter/Facebook (❔ sparse)**, and relies on community contributions on **TikTok/YouTube (⚠️ indirect presence)**. This suggests opportunity (and need) for the brand to grow its direct audience on emerging platforms, as discussed later in Opportunities.
