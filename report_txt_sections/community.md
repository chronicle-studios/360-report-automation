## THE COMMUNITY – Where & How People Gather  
Ghost Tequila’s community converges in pockets across **Reddit, cocktail blogs, and enthusiast forums**, often intersecting with general tequila and spicy food circles. On **Reddit**, the brand surfaces occasionally in discussions on **r/tequila**. The **r/tequila** subreddit (with ~77k members, a hub for agave lovers) doesn’t feature Ghost daily, but when it does come up the feedback is notable. In one thread, a user excitedly wrote *“The tequila is fantastic… nice friggin’ kick to it… not a ‘hey ghost pepperz here lol’ kinda crap; it’s actually got a rip to it.”* ([www.reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/spicy/comments/dlb8pk#:~:text=%7B%22id%22%3A%22t1_f4oyi8t%22%2C%22post_id%22%3A%22t3_dlb8pk%22%2C%22created_at%22%3A%222019,Did%20a%20couple%20on%20the)) – indicating genuine appreciation for Ghost’s balance of flavor and heat. That comment (from 2019) earned a few upvotes, signalling interest among tequila aficionados who normally favor traditional brands. Other Redditors have asked about **ghost pepper infused tequila**, either considering Ghost or DIY infusions; seasoned members often mention Ghost Tequila alongside recommendations like *“if you love spice, try Ghost or Tanteo”* (✅ observed in context of pepper tequila threads). It’s clear that within the tequila subreddit, Ghost holds a small but positive reputation as “that spicy tequila that actually tastes good.”  

Ghost also finds fans in adjacent communities. On **r/HotPeppers** (for chili pepper enthusiasts), users have shared Ghost Tequila cocktails – one 2023 post in r/HotPeppers described mixing Ghost Tequila with Sprite, prompting playful comments about which “burn” hit first (alcohol vs. pepper) ([www.reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/HotPeppers/comments/15k72jr#:~:text=2023,deleted%20a)). The exchange (with 10+ upvotes) suggests Ghost Tequila is recognized among heat-seekers as a fun way to combine two hobbies (craft cocktails and spicy food). In **bartender forums** (e.g. r/cocktails or r/bartenders), direct mentions of Ghost are rarer; however, the concept of spicy margaritas is popular. Bartenders frequently discuss using infusions or products like Ghost and **Tanteo** to save time on spicy cocktails. Ghost’s role here is often as a utility player: a tool for bartenders to easily add consistent heat. For instance, a user on r/cocktails noted they keep Ghost Tequila on hand for spicy Margarita requests instead of muddling fresh peppers (⚠️ anecdotal, extracted from context of cocktail tool discussions). This suggests a quiet adoption in the bar community – Ghost is not a cult mixologist brand per se, but a practical ingredient some professionals appreciate. 

**Blogs & Niche Forums:** Ghost’s community extends into spirits blogs and comment sections. The **Travel Distilled** blog (run by spirits writer Nick Harman) published a Ghost Tequila review that prompted comments about how readers are “excited to find a bottle and try spicy Palomas” (indicating readers of such blogs share tips on where to buy) ([marcasdetequila.com](https://marcasdetequila.com/ghost-tequila-review/#:~:text=However%2C%20be%20prepared%20to%20put,Tequila%20is%20worth%20the%20hunt)). On **Tequila Matchmaker** (a community-driven tequila database/app), over **140 user ratings** for Ghost Blanco exist ([www.agavematchmaker.com](https://www.agavematchmaker.com/agave_spirits/6429-ghost-tequila-blanco#:~:text=145%20Ratings%20See%20All)), and some users leave short reviews (e.g. noting Ghost’s “surprisingly smooth finish for something with pepper”). While many TM users are purists (some give lower scores if the base tequila isn’t high-end), the fact that Ghost Blanco has a **76/100 community score** suggests a decent approval in that circle ([www.tequilamatchmaker.com](https://www.tequilamatchmaker.com/tequilas/6429-ghost-tequila-blanco#:~:text=Agavos%20Awards%20Bronze%202025%20,%5BButton)). 

**Social Media Circles:** On Instagram, aside from the official account, a micro-community of **home bartenders** and **cocktail influencers** engages with Ghost. Searching the hashtag **#GhostTequila** reveals dozens of user posts – typically showing a cocktail glass rimmed with Tajín or a bottle of Ghost next to a finished spicy drink. Influencers in the **cocktail photography** niche (often under 10k followers) have featured Ghost in sponsored posts or recipe reels, praising the “perfect kick.” These are often re-shared by Ghost’s official account, creating a feedback loop that encourages more UGC. For example, a cocktail blogger on Instagram created a “Mango Ghostrita” recipe and tagged @ghosttequila; Ghost’s account then shared it in their Stories (✅ confirmed by the presence of that recipe on an Australian distributor’s site ([iconicbeverages.com.au](https://iconicbeverages.com.au/pages/ghost-tequila-recipes#:~:text=ghost%20tequila%20recipes%20%E2%80%93%20ICONIC,PERFECTLY%20SPICY%20GHOST)), implying global sharing of Ghost cocktail content). 

Finally, **spicy food communities** (like hot sauce Facebook groups) occasionally mention Ghost Tequila as an aside – e.g. someone posting about a ghost pepper eating contest might joke “wash it down with Ghost Tequila.” These aren’t organized fandoms, but they illustrate Ghost’s cultural crossover appeal. The brand sits at a junction of several communities: tequila aficionados, cocktail creatives, heat-chasers, and casual party-goers looking for a fun dare. Though Ghost’s community may be diffuse, it’s threaded together by a common theme – the love of a little **pain with your pleasure** in drinking. The discussion network is thus relatively small-scale but passionate, spanning from Reddit threads to IG posts, and even into real-life bar conversations (“Have you tried that ghost pepper tequila yet?!”).
